Squish Dash – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Published by Copperleaf Studio

Squish Dash is a casual game. This page explains what information the app uses and what it doesn't.

Information we do not collect

Squish Dash has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or exact location.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores and settings (such as haptics) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store them on any server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the menu, optional rewarded ads that let you continue a round, and occasional full-screen ads between rounds. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, approximate location, and how you interact with ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings.

Children

Squish Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
