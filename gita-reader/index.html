Gita Reader – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Published by Copperleaf Studio

Gita Reader is an app for reading the Bhagavad Gita. This page explains what information the app uses and what it doesn't.

Information we do not collect

Gita Reader has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or exact location.

Information stored on your device

Your reading progress, highlights, bookmarks and display settings (theme, text size, translation) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store them on any server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Usage and crash data

We use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand which screens are used and to find and fix crashes. This may collect an app-instance identifier, the screens you open, app version, device model, operating system version, approximate location (country or region, from your IP address), and technical details of crashes. It does not include your reading content, highlights or bookmarks, and we do not use it to identify you.

You can turn this off at any time in the app under Settings → Usage & Crash Data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, this data is collected only if you agree in the consent form. Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising

The free version of Gita Reader shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, approximate location, and how you interact with ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads or collecting usage and crash data. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the privacy option in the app's Settings.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Premium (which removes ads), the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. We may use a subscription service provider to confirm whether your subscription is active.

Children

Gita Reader is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
